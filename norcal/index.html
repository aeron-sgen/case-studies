THE SOLUTION: AN SGEN SITE WITH THE MENU NATIVE, PLUS LOCAL SEO FOR A DELIVERY FOOTPRINT

Two deliverables from the standard suite ran on this account. Nothing bespoke was invented for it.

1. A MENU-CONNECTED WEBSITE ON SGEN, CUSTOMER DATA OWNED BY THE DISPENSARY

NorCal's site was built on SGEN — the platform every one of Dispenza's 500+ dispensary sites runs on — with the menu rendered as native, indexable pages on norcalholistics.org rather than an embed. That is the cannabis website design service in its normal form, with the dispensary menu integration connected to the operator's ordering stack.

"Taken ownership of their own customer data" means something concrete here: a first-party customer list on the dispensary's own platform, built from site orders and sign-ups, exportable, and not rented from a marketplace. The list is the operator's asset. It is the prerequisite for every retention channel that follows.

2. LOCAL SEO SCOPED TO A DELIVERY SERVICE AREA

Delivery SEO is not storefront SEO with the address removed. The dispensary SEO work on this account followed the delivery pattern:

  • Service-area Google Business Profile. The profile is set up as a service-area business, with the served cities declared and the address hidden, so the local pack can show a delivery operator for "dispensary near me" and "dispensary delivery" searches inside that area. This is the dispensary local SEO and Google Business Profile management service.
  • Localized on-page pages. One page per served city or district, each with real service copy, hours, order links and the menu categories that city buys, so the site can rank where there is no storefront to rank.
  • Off-page profiles. The 100+ off-page SEO profiles from the standard plan, built with a consistent name, phone and service-area footprint, so the delivery brand has the citation base a storefront gets for free.
  • Share of Local Voice as the scoreboard. SoLV for "Dispensary Near Me" is the metric Dispenza tracks on every case page: the share of local-pack visibility across the service area, given with its window.

WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT DONE

  • No marketplace ad-buying. Managing a Weedmaps budget is not a Dispenza service; the goal of the work was to make that budget optional.
  • No paid product ads. Major ad platforms restrict paid cannabis promotion under their own policies, so the plan was built for organic and local rank to carry the traffic.
  • No timeline promise beyond the 90-day window reported here.
