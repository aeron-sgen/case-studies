HOW TO READ THE MAPS

Every ranking card is a Google Maps result we measured, not a claim.

Board
One area’s leaderboard for one search: a state, a county or a city. “#1 of 684 in Maine” means first of the 684 listings that reach the top 3 there.
Share
How much of the area’s map shows the store in the top 3. Three listings share every top 3, so a board’s shares add up to three full maps.
Around
The store’s own city and county, plus towns within 30 miles and counties within 45 miles.
Our #1 spots
The 47 count each store at #1 in its own city and county, plus each state it tops. They use the board for all three searches combined.
Town counts
A ranking card counts every town and county where the business is #1 or top 3 for any one of the three searches.
Ranking cards
Each one names the business and where its stores are, then the spots they hold.
