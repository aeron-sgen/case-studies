WHAT WE BUILT

3 stores, one plan: each store's Google profile and listings worked county by county, until each one led its county.

LOCALIZED ON-PAGE SEO

Each location has its own page with its address, hours, map and local copy. Titles, headings and structured data are written per store, so Google matches each page to its own pin. Dispensary local SEO.

100+ OFF-PAGE SEO (PROFILES)

More than one hundred business profiles and listings per location, each matching the Google Business Profile exactly. Listings that agree are how Google confirms the store is where the map says.

GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE MANAGEMENT

Categories, hours, photos, posts and questions are managed on each profile, and reviews are asked for and answered. The profile is what shows in the local pack, so it gets the most care. Google Business Profile for dispensaries: what we manage.

MONTHLY + QUARTERLY ADVANCED REPORTING & ATTRIBUTION

Every month Black Bear Hemp gets the same report: what ran, what it cost, what it returned. The yardstick is Share of Local Voice, the share of the map around each store that shows it in the top 3.
